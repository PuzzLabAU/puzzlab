Welcome to Noir City, where a nefarious mastermind known as Dr DeCrypt has just announced his boldest scheme yet: one theft a day, every day, for fifty days straight. He's cocky enough to leave clues behind each time.

That's where you come in.

As a Noir City detective, it's your job to follow every clue, crack over fifty hand-illustrated puzzles, recover what's been stolen, and finally bring Dr DeCrypt to justice. Every puzzle in the book was designed and illustrated by a real escape room designer.

Our Studio

Hi, I'm Josh! My background's in escape room design, and I studied video game design and development at university. PuzzLab began during COVID as a way to bring escape rooms online so friends could keep playing together - and it grew from there into its own indie game studio.

Our goal is simple! Make the games we want to play, with satisfying puzzles and bloody good stories.
